Customer Support View
You can look up why a customer's transaction was declined or challenged. You will see plain-English reasons only — no internal fraud scores, rule codes, or ML details, as sharing those could help fraudsters game the system.
Pre-approved call scripts for common scenarios. These explain the situation to customers without revealing internal fraud logic.
DECLINED Customer says: "My card was declined and I don't know why"
1
Acknowledge and empathise
"I completely understand how frustrating that must have been, especially at the moment of payment. Let me look into this for you right away."
2
Explain without exposing fraud logic
"Our system flagged this transaction as unusual based on activity on your account. This is an automatic safety measure to protect you from potential fraud. It doesn't mean you did anything wrong."
3
Offer resolution
"I can unlock this for you right now. Could you confirm [verification question]? Once verified, I'll whitelist this merchant for you and you can retry the transaction."
CHALLENGED Customer says: "I got an OTP I didn't ask for"
1
Reassure — this is a security feature, not an error
"That OTP was sent by us as an extra security check. We noticed something slightly unusual about this transaction — a new device or location — and wanted to make sure it was really you."
2
Confirm it was them
"Was the transaction at [merchant name] for [amount] made by you? … Great, then you're all set. The OTP just needed to be entered to approve it."
3
Prevent repeat friction
"To avoid this in future, you can set up trusted devices in your banking app. That way we'll recognise your phone and won't need to send OTPs for routine transactions."
BLOCKED Customer says: "My card has been blocked and I can't use it"
1
Do not confirm fraud suspicion directly
"Your card has been temporarily placed on hold as a precautionary measure. This is done automatically to protect your account. I want to make sure your funds are safe."
2
Verify identity before any action
"Before I can review the restriction, I need to verify your identity. Could you please confirm [2FA verification]. Thank you."
3
Route appropriately
If genuine customer confirmed: escalate to fraud analyst for unblock review. Do NOT unblock directly from support without analyst sign-off when a critical case is flagged.
